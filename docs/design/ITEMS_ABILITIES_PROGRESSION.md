# Items, Abilities and Progression Catalogue — First Draft

> **Exploratory content catalogue, not a locked implementation roster.**
>
> This document preserves the first broad brainstorm for pet-themed RPG items,
> equipment, character actions and progression. It follows the canonical combat
> pillars in [COMBAT_DESIGN.md](COMBAT_DESIGN.md): Instinct, Zoom and
> Companionship. Final mechanics, numbers and content records should only be
> promoted when the active vertical slice requires them.

## Guiding principle

> Everything is an ordinary pet object or behaviour, interpreted by Mango and
> Cooper as something enormously important.

This is the source of the game's EarthBound-like comedy. Items should remain
recognisable parts of pet life even when the pets understand them as legendary
weapons, magic, armour or treasure.

## Character combat identities

### Mango — the strange cat mage

Mango is not a human wizard in a cat costume. His magic comes from inexplicable
cat behaviour made supernatural by the awakened world.

- High Mystery and Speed; comparatively fragile.
- Excels at debuffs, elemental effects and battlefield manipulation.
- Sometimes unreliable, self-interested or chaotic.
- Gradually learns protective and healing behaviour as his emotional arc opens.
- Uses fishing rods as mage tools.

Possible action families:

| Family | Fictional basis |
| --- | --- |
| Fishing-Rod Magic | String, lures, pulling, binding and direct magic |
| Catcraft | Sneaking, staring, pouncing and knocking things over |
| Street Wisdom | Survival skills from Mango's earlier life |
| Affection | Protection and healing learned through relationships |

Candidate actions:

- **Fishing-Rod Flick** — basic magical strike with the lure.
- **Judgemental Stare** — lowers an enemy's attack.
- **Reel In** — pulls or interrupts an exposed enemy.
- **Red-Dot Prophecy** — marks a target for a powerful follow-up.
- **Midnight Gallop** — several chaotic strikes against random enemies.
- **Countertop Catastrophe** — knocks an unidentified object onto all enemies.
- **Bag Ambush** — disappears and becomes temporarily untargetable.
- **Street Cat Sense** — reveals weaknesses and hidden rewards.
- **Nine-Lives Lie** — survives a fatal hit with 1 HP.
- **Solar Nap** — restores health and Mystery while Mango sleeps.
- **Hairball Hex** — lowers enemy accuracy or defence.
- **Unblinking Vigil** — protects Cooper from an incoming attack.
- **Soft Paw** — a later-story heal whose importance is emotional, not only numerical.
- **I Meant to Do That** — turns a miss into a strange beneficial side effect.

Candidate magical flavours are **Sun**, **Shadow**, **Static**, **Plant** and
**String**, rather than a purely generic fire/ice/lightning set.

Fishing-rod equipment ideas:

- Frayed Toy Rod
- Bell-Lure Rod
- Feather Wand
- Forbidden String
- Telescopic Couch Rod
- Rod of the Red Dot
- Vacuum-Cord Rod, made from Mi-chan
- The Uncatchable Lure

### Cooper — the anxious knight

Cooper is a knight because he acts while scared, not because he lacks fear.

- High Heart and Guard.
- Strong physical attacks, protection, interruption and party support.
- Tracks, retrieves and redirects enemies.
- Becomes especially effective while protecting friends or overcoming fear.
- Uses toys held in his mouth as weapons and harnesses as armour.

Possible action families:

| Family | Fictional basis |
| --- | --- |
| Good Boy | Protection, healing and party support |
| Squeak Arts | Toy-based physical attacks |
| Nosework | Tracking, weaknesses and item discovery |
| Courage | Stronger actions while afraid, hurt or protecting someone |

Candidate actions:

- **Bark** — basic startling attack; also the leading starting Instinct.
- **Squeaky Reckoning** — repeatedly bashes an enemy with a favourite toy.
- **Shake It!** — grabs and shakes a small enemy.
- **Fetch** — may retrieve a thrown consumable after use.
- **Protect Mango** — intercepts an attack aimed at Mango.
- **Suspicious Noise** — warns the party and increases evasion.
- **Sniff Out** — reveals weaknesses, drops or disguises.
- **Lead the Way** — increases party speed despite visible nervousness.
- **Leash Lash** — damages and binds.
- **Harness Up** — raises defence while lowering speed.
- **Emergency Cuddle** — heals and removes fear.
- **Brave Anyway** — becomes stronger while Spooked.
- **Absolutely Not** — resists forced movement or instant defeat.
- **Doorbell Apocalypse** — a deafening all-enemy bark barrage.
- **Little Legs, Big Charge** — slow preparation, enormous hit.
- **Where's Mum?** — a powerful emotional action affecting both pets' Zoom.

Toy-weapon ideas:

- Beloved Squeaker
- Slightly Damp Rope
- Unreasonably Loud Chicken
- Half a Tennis Ball
- Indestructible Kong
- Mystery Sock
- The Sacred Slipper
- Milo's Favourite Toy

## Item families

### Meals — major recovery

Budget Kibble; Fancy Kibble; Cat Kibble; Dog Kibble; Suspiciously Expensive
Kibble; Chicken Wet Food; Tuna Wet Food; Gravy-Lovers Pouch; Half a Human
Dinner; Emergency Roast Chicken; The Bottom of the Bowl.

Species affinity can add flavour without becoming fiddly: cat food favours
Mango but is unusually attractive to Cooper; dog food favours Cooper; human food
is powerful but risky; shared food is reliable.

### Treats — small recovery and buffs

Training Treat; Crunchy Biscuit; Liver Treat; Dental Stick; Churu Tube; Dried
Fish; Cheese Cube followed by Regret; Contraband Chicken; Treat Crumbs; Treat
From a Stranger.

### Plants and grass — strange status items

- **Ordinary Grass** — removes a negative status, with a risk of Queasy.
- **Long Grass** — restores a little Heart and Mystery.
- **Spider-Plant Leaf** — produces a random magical effect.
- **Catnip** — raises Mango's power and speed while reducing control.
- **Lemon Grass** — removes sleep or confusion; Mango dislikes it.
- **Forbidden Houseplant** — huge Mystery recovery with an unknown side effect.
- **Perfect Patch of Sun-Warmed Grass** — full recovery outside battle.
- **Mystery Weed** — “Mango knows what this is. He won't explain.”

Possible grass outcomes: Refreshed, Queasy, Hairball, Inspired, Zoomy, or
Absolutely Nothing.

### Water and comfort

Water Bowl; Cold Water Bowl; Forbidden Glass of Human Water; Puddle Water; Warm
Blanket; Favourite Bed; Human-Scented Shirt; Patch of Sunlight; Fresh Laundry.

Comfort items can remove Spooked or Lonely and reinforce the story's emotional
centre rather than serving only as conventional potions.

### Toys — consumables and battle tools

Tennis Ball; Red Dot; Cat Spring; Crinkle Ball; Cardboard Tube; Feather; Empty
Box; Squeaky Toy; Piece of String; Bubble Wand; Sock; Toilet Roll.

Toys should interact with character identity: Cooper may Fetch thrown items;
string may bind Mango as well as the enemy; an Empty Box can create cover.

### Strange treasures and quest objects

Receipt From the Vet; One Perfect Crumb; Dead Remote Control; Very Important
Bottle Cap; Feather of Unclear Origin; Crumpled Grocery Bag; The Good Cardboard
Box; A Different Cardboard Box; Half a Photograph; Tiny Human Sock; Smell From
Yesterday; Apology Note From the Vacuum; Object Under the Fridge.

Some can be useless, some quest-critical and some inexplicably powerful. Their
descriptions carry much of the humour.

> **Object Under the Fridge**  
> Nobody remembers what it was. It has grown strong in darkness.

## Equipment model

Keep equipment deliberately light so inventory management does not swallow the
characters.

| Slot | Mango | Cooper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tool | Fishing rod | Mouth toy |
| Wearable | Collar, cape or hoodie | Harness, collar or coat |
| Treasure | Bell, tag, charm, sock or keepsake | Tag, charm, sock or keepsake |

Wearable ideas include Purple Mage Hoodie, Bell Collar, Flea Collar of
Protection, Post-Surgery Cone, Raincoat, Reflective Harness, Too-Tight Jumper,
Thunder Shirt, Council Registration Tag and Bandana of Questionable Authority.

The **Post-Surgery Cone** should provide enormous defence and terrible accuracy.

## Candidate status effects

- **Zoomy** — increased speed; behaviour may become automatic.
- **Spooked** — reduced confidence or a chance to hesitate.
- **Tangled** — movement or position change is blocked.
- **Queasy** — minor damage after acting.
- **Distracted** — may target the wrong thing.
- **Wet** — changes speed and Static interactions.
- **Muddy** — lowers accuracy but improves resistance.
- **Cone of Shame** — defence up, accuracy down.
- **Good Boy** — strengthens the next support action.
- **Catnip** — Mystery and speed up, judgement down.
- **Lonely** — reduces healing received; use sparingly and meaningfully.
- **Comforted** — resists fear and loneliness.
- **Hairball** — increases skill costs until cleared.

## Progression direction

Avoid a large branching stat tree. These named characters should not become
interchangeable builds.

1. Experience automatically raises a small set of core stats.
2. Story events, objects and relationships unlock actions.
3. Equipment changes tactical emphasis.
4. A few milestone choices specialise each pet without creating a huge tree.
5. Milestone choices should be reversible while resting at home.
6. Equippable Instincts remain governed by the canonical combat design.

Suggested player-facing stats:

- **Heart** — HP
- **Power** — physical damage
- **Mystery** — magical and support strength
- **Guard** — defence
- **Speed** — turn order and evasion
- **Luck** — critical hits and strange outcomes

Candidate Mango emphases: **Street Cat**, **House Mage**, **Soft Centre**.

Candidate Cooper emphases: **Squeak Knight**, **Goodest Boy**,
**Brave Anyway**.

These are emphasis packages, not large permanent trees.

## Companionship actions

Companionship should unlock pair-specific behaviour rather than generic
percentage bonuses.

- **Cat and Dog Routine** — Mango distracts; Cooper charges.
- **You Check First** — Cooper protects while Mango reveals weaknesses.
- **Shared Bowl** — one healing item affects both pets.
- **Back-to-Back** — both resist fear.
- **He's With Me** — Mango reduces hostility toward Cooper.
- **No One Gets Left Behind** — revives a fallen partner at low HP.
- **Home Is Us** — late-game protection against Loneliness.

The progression fantasy is two animals who initially barely understand each
other gradually becoming a family.

## Zoomies identity

Zoomies remain automatic, personality-driven consequences of repeatedly
embracing Instinct:

- **Mango — 3 A.M. Catastrophe:** chaotic attacks, knocked-over scenery and one
  accidental party benefit.
- **Cooper — Ultimate Squeaky Rampage:** repeated toy strikes ending beside the
  lowest-Heart ally in a guarding stance.
- **Rocky — Perpetual Motion:** permanently Zoomy, devastating and impossible to
  command cleanly.

Mango's Zoomies should feel chaotic and magical. Cooper's should begin as panic
and end as protection.

## First combat-content test

Do not implement the full catalogue at once. The smallest useful identity test
is:

- Item: **Cat Kibble**
- Mango basic: **Fishing-Rod Flick**
- Mango command skill: **Judgemental Stare**
- Mango Instinct candidate: **Pounce**
- Cooper basic/Instinct candidate: **Bark**
- Cooper relationship action: **Protect Mango**
- Enemy: **Dust Bunny**
- Statuses: **Spooked** and **Distracted**
- Equipment: **Frayed Toy Rod** and **Beloved Squeaker**

This test should prove that Mango manipulates and humiliates enemies, Cooper
attacks but instinctively protects, food feels species-specific, ordinary toys
read as grand RPG equipment, and the Instinct/Zoom loop remains legible.

## Open decisions

- Which catalogue entries graduate from joke to real mechanic?
- Does Mystery appear as a player-facing resource name or only as flavour?
- Which status set is small enough for the first real battle?
- Are milestone emphases necessary once equippable Instincts are playable?
- Which items are shared, species-affine or character-exclusive?
- Is Mango's starting Instinct definitely Pounce?
- How does Cooper's Bark work both as a basic identity action and a contextual
  Instinct without confusing the command menu?

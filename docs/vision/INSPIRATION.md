# Inspiration

Reference material for art direction, tone, and structure — separate from [`../art/ART_BIBLE.md`](../art/ART_BIBLE.md) (which holds the *locked decisions*, e.g. the 16×20 north/south, 20×16 east/west, 32×32 and 40×40 sprite scales, palette rules). This doc is the raw inspiration feeding those decisions, not a source of truth itself. Update it as new references come up in conversation; don't let it go stale.

## Thematic / Structural References

Not visual references — these are for tone and narrative structure. Worth playing rather than just screenshotting.

- **Costume Quest** (Double Fine, 2010) — trick-or-treating kids imagine full RPG battles in their own neighborhood; the mundane world becomes the fantasy world through a kid's imagination. Closest structural cousin to this project's entire premise.
- **Rakuen** (Laura Shigihara, 2017) — a hospitalized child's world transforms into the bedtime stories his mother tells him. Similar structural move to Costume Quest, but the reference here is tone: unusually sincere, doesn't undercut its emotional beats with irony. Closest tonal reference for Charlie's arc specifically (see CLAUDE.md's note on keeping that arc sincere, not played for shock or cheap sentiment).
- **Bug Fables** (2020) — ants and beetles treat their own backyard as a continent-spanning kingdom. Proof that "the adventure map is literally your house/neighbourhood, recontextualized" can carry a full JRPG-scale game without feeling small.

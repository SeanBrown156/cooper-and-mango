## Identity and likeness

- Cavalier King Charles Spaniel.
- Ruddy chestnut/copper patches on a clean white body.
- Freckled white-and-brown muzzle and long silky ears.
- Colour-linked to Mango's orange family, but redder and richer.

## Personality and behaviour cues

- Berserker ally with immense, uncontrollable power.
- Bravery must not be confused with recklessness.
- Power reads in posture even at rest; do not make Rocky's silhouette small or dainty merely because of the breed.

## Identity and likeness

- Cavoodle with a black, grey and warm cream coat; the undertone must remain ruddy, never cold-grey.
- Shaggy, grizzled muzzle and beard.
- Warm cream/tan eyebrow markings, chest and paw socks against the darker body.
- Preserve real ear shape, coat, paw markings, curled tail and open expressive face.

## Personality and behaviour cues

- Earnest and anxious.
- Demure, brave and defiant; often looks helpless or worried while refusing to give up.
- Anxiety-to-courage should read through posture, expression and animation. The Game Bible remains authoritative for the arc.

## Art direction

- Keep the face open and expressive rather than stern or conventionally heroic.
- Battle poses communicate a melee character who is brave despite visible uncertainty.
- Upright/bipedal battle and portrait poses must remain distinct from the quadruped overworld grammar.

## Role-specific visual grammar

- Overworld: 20×16, quadruped low-top-down, four cardinal directions, consistent footprint and baseline.
- Battle: 32×32, primarily standing or seated upright on two legs; quadruped attacks are a minority.
- Portrait: 40×40, expressive upright likeness with face, muzzle, ears and accessory detail.

## Signature items and props

- The green object is a soft, inflated, chunky tubular loop with dark emerald fabric and repeating white swirl print.
- It is not a flat scarf, bandana, ordinary collar, rope or generic green accessory.
- It may appear as a playful melee prop: bite/gnaw tool, lasso/loop, swing, toy sword-like object or toy knuckle-duster.

## Animation invariants

- Preserve the black/grey/warm-cream coat, floppy ears, cream muzzle/paws, curled tail and tubular green object.
- Keep head and feet/baseline relationships stable through attacks and motion.
- Battle animations preserve the upright/seated-upright melee identity where appropriate.
- Overworld animation remains quadruped and four-directional.
